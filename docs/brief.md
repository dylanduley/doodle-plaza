# Product brief

## Problem

Most social media rewards polish: filtered photos, perfect captions, follower counts. Posting feels high-stakes, so many people mostly watch. Nintendo's Miiverse and Flipnote Hatena showed a different model: when every post is a quick hand-drawn doodle on a tiny canvas, the bar for posting drops and the feed feels personal and playful. Both services are gone, and nothing on the web fills the same niche.

## Who it's for

- **Casual doodlers** who like drawing but would never post to an art site, where the quality bar feels intimidating.
- **People nostalgic for Miiverse and Flipnote.**
- **Hobbyist animators** who want a low-effort way to make short, loopable frame-by-frame animations.

## Core idea

Everything is drawn. Your profile picture is a drawing. Every post is a drawing, either a still or a short animation, with an optional short caption. Replies can be drawn or typed.

## Constraints (intentional)

- **Small, wide canvas (320×120)** keeps drawings quick and files tiny.
- **Limited palette (paper + 3 inks)** keeps the feed visually consistent and lowers the pressure to make something "good."
- **Hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing** makes the fill bucket reliable and gives the app its look.

## Scope for v1 (two-week build)

**In:** drawing editor, animation editor (frames, onion skin, playback), sign-up with a drawn profile picture, posting, a simple feed, empty/error states, basic reporting.

**Out (listed as future work):** follows, notifications, communities, sound, comments threads.

## Success looks like

- A first-time visitor can draw and post something in under a minute.
- Testers can make a short animation without instructions.
- The editor feels responsive on desktop and phone.
